Use case: style-transfer.

Input image: the previous colorful controller app icon is the edit target. Preserve its recognizable game-controller concept and central balanced composition, but comprehensively simplify its visual treatment as requested.

Subject: a single compact white game-controller glyph, elegant smooth rounded silhouette. One simple medium-blue rounded plus on the left and four small identical blue circles in a precise 2x2 grid on the right. All controls use the same blue. Remove top triggers, recessed circular rings, extra seams, and all secondary details.
Style: minimalist polished mobile OS icon, almost flat with very subtle translucent milky-white depth and a delicate light-catching upper edge. Composition: frontal orthographic view, exact optical center, compact controller occupies about 64 percent of canvas width, broad safe margins, balanced left and right. One icon only.
Palette: blue and white ONLY, including pale blue for soft shading. No black, gray, yellow, coral, green, purple or rainbow colors.
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, watermark, logos, sparkles, particles, decorative rings, extra objects, mockup or border. Fully opaque background, sharp clean contours, professional minimal design.
